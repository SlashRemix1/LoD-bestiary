HP and EXP

Official Nexon game_info/monster/list.asp only. Wayback captured 우드랜드 (2 pages), 지하묘지, 포테의숲, and 죽음의마을. Column A is HP and column B is EXP. A official “?” is left blank. Every other area’s HP and EXP are blank. Darkstory, quick-dungeon, and Dark Ages numbers are not used.

A Korean-archive pass (official board, 성천직자 cafe via board 7056, Namu/DC/thewiki, Naver blogs, Wayback) found no classic habitat Sense dump. The posts that list numbers are quick-dungeon brackets, S-solo, or Nageling, so they were left out. See SOURCES.md.
